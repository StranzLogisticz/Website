## Fleet Expansion

Stranz Logistics has added new **32ft MXL container trucks** to our heavy-duty fleet. These are the largest closed-body vehicles in our network, built for maximum-tonnage long-haul operations.

## Vehicle Specifications

| Spec | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Body Length | 32 feet |
| Body Type | MXL (Multi-axle Extra Large) |
| Payload Capacity | Up to 18 Tons |
| Body Style | Fully Closed |
| Best Use | Long-haul, heavy industrial freight |

## Why MXL?

MXL containers offer the highest load volume in the closed-body category. Key advantages:

- **Maximum cargo protection** — fully sealed body with tamper-evident locks
- **Higher tonnage per trip** — reduces the number of trips needed
- **Cost efficiency** — better per-unit freight economics at high volumes
- **Long-distance rated** — designed for 600+ km interstate runs

## Active Routes

The new 32ft MXL units are operational on:

- Chennai → Bengaluru (via NH-48)
- Chennai → Coimbatore (via NH-544)
- Coimbatore → Hosur industrial corridor
- Bengaluru → Hyderabad (on request)

## Availability

Fleet availability for the new MXL units is on a contract and spot basis. Industrial clients with recurring high-volume requirements are eligible for dedicated vehicle allocation.
