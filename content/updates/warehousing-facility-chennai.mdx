## New Facility

Stranz Logistics has commissioned a new **SOC-compliant warehousing facility** in North Chennai, strategically located near the NH-16 corridor with direct access to Chennai Port and the Northern bypass.

The facility adds **15,000 square feet** of structured storage capacity to our network.

## Facility Highlights

- **Area:** 15,000 sq ft (expandable to 22,000 sq ft)
- **Location:** North Chennai, near Ennore–Manali industrial zone
- **Compliance:** SOC and SOP-compliant with documented handling procedures
- **Security:** 24/7 CCTV, access-controlled entry, fire suppression system
- **Connectivity:** 500m from NH-16, 8km from Chennai Port gate

## Storage Capabilities

The facility is designed to handle:

- **FMCG palletised storage** — racking systems up to 5-tier
- **Industrial goods** — bulk floor storage with heavy equipment access
- **Bonded storage** — pre-clearance goods pending customs documentation
- **Temperature-ambient storage** — maintained between 18°C–28°C

## Integrated Services

This is not a standalone warehouse — it is fully integrated with Stranz's transport network:

- Daily inbound from port and supplier locations
- Daily outbound to retail distribution points across South India
- Inventory management with digital stock reports
- Inbound/outbound coordination by dedicated warehouse staff
