## What Is e-POD?

An **electronic Proof of Delivery (e-POD)** is a digital record generated the moment a shipment is delivered and confirmed at the destination. It replaces paper-based delivery receipts entirely.

Starting April 2025, every shipment handled by Stranz Logistics now comes with an auto-generated e-POD delivered directly to the client.

## How It Works

1. Driver arrives at the delivery point
2. Receiver confirms delivery digitally (signature or OTP)
3. e-POD is generated instantly with timestamp, location, and receiver details
4. Client receives the e-POD via email within minutes
5. Invoice is auto-triggered based on delivery confirmation

## Benefits for Our Clients

- **Faster billing cycles** — no waiting for physical documents
- **Zero paperwork** — all PODs stored digitally and retrievable anytime
- **Dispute resolution** — timestamped, GPS-verified delivery records
- **Audit-ready** — exportable records for your accounts team

## Digital Invoicing

Alongside e-POD, our invoicing has moved fully digital. Clients now receive:

- GST-compliant digital invoices via email
- Monthly consolidated statements
- Itemised billing per route and vehicle type

The e-POD system is now live across **all active routes**. If you are an existing client and have not yet received your login credentials for the client portal, contact your coordinator.
