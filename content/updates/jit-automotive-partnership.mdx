## Partnership Announcement

Stranz Logistics has entered into a **Just-In-Time (JIT) logistics agreement** with a major Tier-1 automotive components supplier based in the Oragadam industrial cluster, Chennai.

Under this contract, Stranz will manage the inbound and outbound freight for precision engine components, transmission parts, and sub-assemblies.

## What Is JIT Logistics?

Just-In-Time logistics means cargo arrives **exactly when it is needed** on the production line — not early, not late. There is no buffer stock. The logistics provider must:

- Execute to precise time windows (often ±30 minutes)
- Maintain zero missed pickups and deliveries
- Provide real-time status updates to the client's ERP
- Operate dedicated vehicles on fixed schedules

## Our Commitment

To support this contract, Stranz has:

- Allocated dedicated vehicles solely for this client
- Assigned a dedicated logistics coordinator available 24/7
- Integrated GPS tracking with client-side ERP notifications
- Established a contingency protocol for any delay scenarios

## Significance

This partnership represents Stranz's entry into **precision automotive logistics** — one of the most demanding segments in supply chain management. Successfully operating JIT contracts requires a level of operational discipline that validates our overall service quality.

## About Oragadam

Oragadam is home to some of India's largest automotive OEMs and their Tier-1 suppliers, including Renault-Nissan, Komatsu, and Apollo Tyres. Stranz now services multiple facilities within this industrial zone.
