## Full Fleet GPS Coverage

As of January 2025, **every active vehicle in the Stranz Logistics fleet** is equipped with live GPS tracking. This marks the completion of our fleet-wide telematics rollout.

## What Clients Get

Clients with active shipments can now:

- **Track in real time** — vehicle location updated every 2 minutes
- **Get checkpoint alerts** — notifications at key route milestones
- **Receive ETA updates** — estimated delivery time recalculated live
- **Access delivery confirmation** — instant notification on arrival

## What We Monitor Internally

Our operations team uses the GPS data for:

- Speed monitoring and driver safety compliance
- Geofence breach alerts (if a vehicle leaves the approved corridor)
- Idle time tracking to improve efficiency
- Historical route analysis for optimisation

## Technology Stack

Our GPS system uses a combination of:

- SIM-based vehicle trackers (OBD + hardwired)
- 4G connectivity with fallback SMS alerts
- Cloud-based dashboard accessible by coordinators and clients
- API integration available for enterprise clients

## Why This Matters

Before this rollout, tracking was partial and manual. Now, every stakeholder — client, coordinator, and driver — operates with full visibility. This directly reduces delay disputes, improves driver accountability, and makes our SLA reporting more reliable.

## For Existing Clients

If you'd like access to the live tracking dashboard for your shipments, contact your assigned coordinator. Access is included at no additional charge for all active contract clients.
