## Overview

Stranz Logistics has officially expanded its operational coverage to include the **Coimbatore–Hosur Industrial Corridor** — one of the fastest-growing manufacturing belts in South India.

This corridor is home to a dense cluster of auto-component manufacturers, electronics assembly units, and precision engineering firms. Our new dedicated routes ensure reliable, scheduled freight movement for clients operating across this belt.

## What's New

- Dedicated vehicle allocation on the Coimbatore–Hosur route
- Scheduled daily departures from both ends of the corridor
- Integration with our existing Chennai and Bengaluru network
- GPS-tracked from pickup to delivery confirmation

## Who This Serves

This expansion is designed for:

- **Auto-component manufacturers** shipping to OEM plants in Hosur
- **Electronics firms** in the Coimbatore SIDCO cluster
- **Industrial goods exporters** moving cargo toward Chennai port

## Coverage Points

Our new corridor routes connect the following key industrial zones:

- Coimbatore SIDCO & Peelamedu Industrial Area
- Tiruppur Export Cluster
- Krishnagiri & Hosur Auto Park
- Electronic City, Bengaluru (southbound)
