Our Journey

2018

Foundation Year

Stranz Logistics was founded with a single truck and contract service, driven by a vision to simplify logistics across India.

2019–2020

Growth & Expansion

Expanded fleet size and onboarded new vendors, steadily building customer trust and establishing regional presence.

2021–2022

Infrastructure Advancement

Introduced fully closed trucks and advanced warehousing solutions, ensuring safety, compliance, and quality service.

2023–2024

Digital Transformation

Strengthened GPS tracking and digital platforms, enhancing transparency, real-time updates, and operational efficiency.

2025

Future Vision

Ramping up digitalization, preparing for nationwide expansion, and scaling vendor and customer networks strategically.
